## App name (max 30)
PremDue: Premium Due Tracker

## Short description (max 80)
Turn your premium due list into a daily worklist. Free, private, no account.

## Full description (max 4000)
PremDue turns the monthly premium due list PDF that insurance agents receive into a clear daily worklist, so fewer policies lapse and more renewals come in.

SHARE YOUR DUE LIST, GET YOUR DAY
Open your Premium Due List PDF from email and share it to PremDue. In seconds you see who to call today, whose grace period is ending and which policies are already at risk of lapsing. PremDue checks every row against the PDF's own Grand Total, so a list that does not add up is refused, never shown wrong.

NEVER MISS A RENEWAL
• Lapse risk first: policies grouped into lapse risk, new, maturing and regular, using standard grace periods (15 days for monthly premiums, 30 days for others).
• One-tap follow-up: call, SMS or WhatsApp with the number and a ready reminder in Marathi, Hindi or English.
• Record what happened: paid, promised to pay on a date, or not reachable.
• Daily 9 AM summary: today's dues, promises and policies close to lapsing.

KNOW YOUR MONTH
• Collections and commission this month.
• Lapse risk by months behind, and commission at risk.
• Dues by week, policy mix and biggest premiums.

SELL BETTER
• Plan presentation: turn a plan illustration into a clean PDF to share with a customer.
• Calculators: policy return (IRR), human life value, retirement, child goal, inflation, growth, loan EMI, and loan + SIP.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics. PremDue does not even use the internet.
• Your customers' data stays on your phone. Backups go only to your own Google account or a file you save.

TRY IT IN ONE TAP
No due list handy? Tap "Try with a sample list" to explore PremDue with fictional customers.

PremDue is free.

PremDue is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) or any insurer.
